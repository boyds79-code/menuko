Getting started

Get your restaurant live on Menuko in 6 steps

Most owners finish this in under half an hour — the menu is usually what takes the longest.

1
Sign up

Create your owner account with your restaurant's name, address, and business type.

2
Add your menu

Create categories, then add items with a price and a photo — snap it right from your phone. Mark your 1–3 best sellers as "Our Best" so they show up first.

3
Pick a design

Choose Terracotta, Heritage, or Nordic in Settings → Menu design, and preview it before you commit to it.

4
Print your table QR codes

Settings → Tables → Print all QR codes. Cut along the borders and tape one to each table.

5
Invite your team

Add a kitchen and a cashier login from Settings so they can start managing orders on their own phones.

6
You're live

A customer scans, orders, and it shows up in your kitchen in real time. That's it.

Good to know: the free plan includes 1 owner + 1 kitchen + 1 cashier login and full access to ordering, kitchen, cashier, and today's sales. Need more staff seats or deeper sales analytics? Try Premium — the first 2 months are free.

Frequently asked

Questions owners ask us before switching

The honest answers — including the couple of things Menuko doesn't do yet.

QDoes Menuko take a cut of my sales?

No commission, ever. Payment still happens your way — cash, GCash, Maya — Menuko never touches your money.

QDo I need to buy any special hardware?

No. Customers use their own phone's camera and browser; your kitchen and cashier can use any phone, tablet, or computer you already have.

QDoes it connect to my existing POS?

Not yet — Menuko currently runs on its own, separate from other POS or accounting software.

QWhat if the internet drops?

A brief drop is fine — the kitchen and cashier apps queue up "served" and "paid" updates and sync once you're back online. Placing a new order always needs a live connection (yours or the customer's), so we recommend offering customer WiFi.

QHow much does it cost?

Ordering, kitchen, cashier, and today's sales are free — 1 owner + 1 kitchen + 1 cashier login included. Need more seats or deeper analytics? Try Premium, free for your first 2 months.

QCan customers pay inside the app?

No — Menuko can show your GCash/Maya QR or payment link, but the actual payment is confirmed with your cashier, same as today.

QCan a customer cancel or change an order?

They can request it right from their phone; your cashier (or you, if you're at the restaurant) approves or denies it.

QIs my data secure?

Yes — encrypted connections and access-controlled data. Full details at menuko.ph/privacy.
